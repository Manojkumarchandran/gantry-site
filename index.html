Questions

Frequently asked

How does a project work with Gantry?

We start with a free call, send a written scope and fixed price, then build in weekly steps with a demo each week. After launch we help with fixes and updates.

Can you improve a site or app I already have?

Yes. We can review what you have, fix problems and add features, or rebuild parts of it.

Do you work with clients outside Theni?

Yes. We work with clients across Tamil Nadu and online with clients elsewhere.

Who owns the code?

You do. When the project is complete, the code and accounts are handed over to you.

Are you hiring?

We are always happy to meet good developers and designers. See the Careers section and send us your profile.
